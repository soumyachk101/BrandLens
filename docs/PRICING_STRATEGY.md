## 1. Pricing Philosophy

BrandLens follows a **value-based, segmented pricing strategy** designed to align price with customer outcomes across three distinct buyer personas. Our pricing reflects:

1. **Value captured**: The higher the brand's revenue at risk from AI invisibility, the more value BrandLens delivers.
2. **Customer willingness-to-pay**: Tiered to capture budget across small agencies through Fortune 500.
3. **Operating costs**: White-label tier offsets higher support and infrastructure costs.
4. **Market positioning**: Positioned below enterprise media-monitoring suites (~$5K-$50K/year) but above hobbyist tools.

**Core Principle:** *Pricing should grow with the customer as they expand usage, brands monitored, and resale revenue.*

## 3. Annual Billing Discounts

### 3.1 Discount Structure

BrandLens offers a **20% discount on annual commitments**, applicable to all three tiers.

| Tier | Monthly Billing | Annual Billing (Monthly Equivalent) | Annual Billing (Upfront Total) | Annual Savings |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------------------|-------------------------------|----------------|
| Agency | $299 | $239 | $2,868 | $720 |
| Enterprise | $999 | $799 | $9,588 | $2,400 |
| White-label | $1,999 | $1,599 | $19,188 | $4,800 |

### 3.2 Why 20% Annual Discount?

The 20% annual discount serves three purposes:

1. **Improves cash flow** — Upfront capital funds operations and growth
2. **Reduces churn** — Annual customers churn at 1/3 the rate of monthly customers
3. **Increases LTV** — Annual commitment increases lifetime value by ~25%

### 3.3 Multi-Year Discounts (Enterprise & White-label Only)

For Enterprise and White-label customers, multi-year discounts are available:

| Commitment | Discount |
|-------------|----------|
| Annual | 20% |
| 2-year | 25% |
| 3-year | 30% |

### 3.4 Annual vs. Monthly Defaults

**Pricing Display Best Practice:**
- Show monthly equivalent as the primary price
- Show annual discount as a banner: *"Save 20% with annual billing"*
- Default to annual in checkout flow (with clear option to switch)

## 4. Enterprise Custom Pricing

### 4.1 When to Customize

Enterprise custom pricing applies to customers whose needs exceed White-label tier limits or who have unique procurement requirements.

| Trigger | Customization |
|---------|---------------|
| Monthly query volume > 50,000 | Volume-based pricing tiers |
| Sub-accounts > 50 | Per-account add-on fee |
| Multi-region data residency | Custom data storage pricing |
| Custom AI models | Add-on per model |
| Dedicated infrastructure | Reserved capacity pricing |
| Custom integrations / development | SOW-based professional services |
| Procurement requirements (custom MSA) | Legal review and negotiated terms |

### 4.2 Enterprise Pricing Models

**Volume-Based:**
- Tiered pricing for queries above 25K/month
- $0.05 per additional query block of 1,000

**Per-Brand Pricing:**
- $40/brand/month for brands beyond 50
- Volume discounts at 100, 250, 500+ brands

**Per-User Pricing:**
- $30/user/month for users beyond 25

**Custom Infrastructure:**
- Reserved capacity: 1.5x standard rate
- Dedicated environment: 2.5x standard rate

### 4.3 Enterprise Proposal Structure

A typical enterprise proposal includes:

1. **Executive Summary** — Customer challenge and solution overview
2. **Pricing Summary** — Custom bundle with annual commitment
3. **Implementation Plan** — Timeline and milestones
4. **Service Level Agreement** — Uptime, support, performance commitments
5. **Security & Compliance** — SOC 2, GDPR, CCPA, data processing terms
6. **Renewal Terms** — Price protection, expansion, and exit clauses

### 4.4 Negotiating Authority

| Deal Size | Approval Authority |
|-----------|-------------------|
| < $25,000/year | Sales Manager |
| $25,000 - $100,000/year | VP of Sales |
| $100,000 - $500,000/year | CEO |
| > $500,000/year | CEO + Board |

### 4.5 Enterprise Customer Examples

**Example 1: Global Fortune 500 Brand Team**

## 7. Pricing Page Design Principles

### 7.1 Three-Column Layout

BrandLens pricing page should display three tiers with:
- Clear feature comparison matrix
- "Most Popular" badge on the Enterprise tier
- Monthly/annual toggle (default to annual with savings callout)
- Prominent CTA for each tier
- Trust signals: customer logos, testimonials, security badges

### 7.2 Pricing Page Components

1. **Headline**: "Pricing that scales with your brand's AI visibility"
2. **Subhead**: "From agencies to Fortune 500 teams — start measuring in minutes"
3. **Toggle**: Monthly | Annual (Save 20%)
4. **Three Tier Cards**: Agency / Enterprise / White-label
5. **Feature Matrix**: Below the cards, full feature comparison
6. **Trust Section**: Customer logos, NPS score, testimonials
7. **FAQ**: Common pricing questions
8. **Custom CTA**: "Need more? Talk to sales"

### 7.3 Conversion Optimization

- Reduce friction: 14-day trial, no credit card required for Agency tier
- Highlight savings: Annual savings callout near the toggle
- Risk reversal: 30-day money-back guarantee on all plans
- Social proof: Customer count, NPS, featured customer logos

## 9. Pricing Evolution Roadmap

### 9.1 Phase 1: Launch (Year 1)

- Three core tiers (Agency, Enterprise, White-label)
- 20% annual discount
- Launch promotions for early adopters
- Standard overage rates

### 9.2 Phase 2: Optimization (Year 2)

Based on customer behavior data:
- [ ] A/B test price points ($249 vs. $299 vs. $349 for Agency)
- [ ] Introduce usage-based pricing tiers
- [ ] Add a Starter tier ($99/month) for product-led growth
- [ ] Implement usage-based alerts and proactive upgrade prompts
- [ ] Test 30% annual discount (vs. 20%) to drive annual commitments

### 9.3 Phase 3: Expansion (Year 3)

- [ ] Industry-specific pricing tiers (Healthcare, Financial Services, etc.)
- [ ] Volume discounts for multi-brand customers
- [ ] Outcome-based pricing pilots (pay-per-improvement)
- [ ] Marketplace and app store pricing for partner integrations
- [ ] International pricing localization (EUR, GBP, JPY)
