This financial model covers **Years 1-3** of BrandLens operations with monthly granularity in the first year and quarterly granularity in Years 2-3. All projections are **bottom-up** based on customer acquisition assumptions, churn models, and operating cost estimates.

## 5. Break-Even Analysis

### 5.1 Break-Even Points

| Scenario | Assumption | Breakeven Revenue | Timeline |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|----------|
| **Conservative** | Slow growth, high churn, $1.2M total funding | ~$6.0M ARR | Year 5 |

| **Optimistic** | Strong growth, low churn, $4.0M total funding | ~$4.5M ARR | Year 4 |

### 5.2 Unit Economics Break-Even

Each customer becomes profitable after **~18 months** (crossover point):

| Month | Cumulative CAC | Cumulative Revenue | Cumulative Margin |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|------------------|
| 1 | $380 | $340 | ($40) |
| 3 | $380 | $1,020 | $646 |

| 12 | $380 | $4,080 | $3,120 |
| 18 | $380 | $6,120 | $4,848 |
| 24 | $380 | $8,160 | $6,576 |

**Break-even per customer:** ~Month 2 (first month of positive contribution margin after CAC)

### 5.3 Breakeven by Revenue Model

| Revenue Model | Customers Needed (Year 3 Blended) | Customers Needed (Agency Only) | Customers Needed (Enterprise Only) |
|--------------|----------------------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| SaaS only | 420 | 1,560 | 670 |
| SaaS + Services | 380 | 1,420 | 605 |

**Interpretation:** At Year 3 ARR of $2.5M with 520 customers, BrandLens is close to revenue-level break-even but not operating-level break-even. The company needs scale or reduced OpEx to reach profitability.

### 8.5 Path to Self-Sustainability

To reach operating cash flow breakeven without additional funding:

| Scenario | Conditions Required |
|----------|---------------------|
| **Best case** | ARR hits $5.0M by Month 36 with gross margins > 85% and OpEx growth slows to 50% of revenue growth |
| **Base case** | ARR hits $6.0M by Month 42; Series A provides sufficient runway to reach breakeven |
| **Worst case** | ARR stalls at $3.0M; requires another bridge round or significant cost reduction |

**Realistic Path:** Series A at $8M+ at Month 36, followed by 18-24 months of runway to reach operating cash flow breakeven at ~$6-8M ARR.
