## 1. Target Segments

BrandLens targets three primary customer segments, each with distinct needs, buying behaviors, and price sensitivity.

### 1.1 Segment A: Marketing Agencies

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Size** | 5-200 employees; $1M-$50M annual revenue |
| **Role** | Full-service digital marketing, content marketing, PPC agencies |
| **Primary Buyer** | Account Director, Head of Growth, Managing Partner |
| **Pain Point** | Cannot report AI brand visibility to clients; losing competitive edge to agencies that can |
| **Budget Authority** | $299-$999/month discretionary spend |
| **Decision Cycle** | 1-2 weeks; often self-serve or light-touch sales |
| **Target Count (Year 1)** | 50-70 agencies |
| **Primary Value** | White-label reporting, competitive benchmarking, client retention |

**Why They Buy:**
- Agencies need to differentiate their service offering
- Clients are asking "how do we appear in AI?" — agencies need answers
- White-label reports make agencies look like they built the tool
- Competitive intelligence is a sticky, high-value feature

**Acquisition Channels:**
- Agency directories (Clutch, DesignRush, AgencySpotter)
- LinkedIn outreach to agency leadership
- Marketing conference sponsorships (MozCon, Social Media Marketing World)
- Referral program: 10% recurring commission for agency-to-agency referrals

### 1.2 Segment B: Fortune 500 Brand Teams

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Size** | Enterprise organizations; $1B+ annual revenue |
| **Role** | In-house marketing, brand, communications, and SEO teams |
| **Primary Buyer** | VP/Director of Brand, Head of SEO, CMO office |
| **Pain Point** | No visibility into AI-generated brand narrative; compliance risk |
| **Budget Authority** | $999-$5,000/month; often requires procurement approval |
| **Decision Cycle** | 2-4 months; security review, vendor evaluation, procurement |
| **Target Count (Year 1)** | 15-25 enterprises |
| **Primary Value** | Audit trails, competitive benchmarking, API access, SLA |

**Why They Buy:**
- AI is becoming a primary brand touchpoint — need governance
- Competitors are visible in AI answers; they need to know if they are too
- Audit trails satisfy legal, compliance, and brand-safety requirements
- Executive dashboards for board-level reporting

**Acquisition Channels:**
- Enterprise sales: outbound to VP/Director level via LinkedIn and email
- Industry conference presence (Forrester B2B Summit, CMO conferences)
- Partner referrals from marketing consultancies
- Content marketing: whitepapers on AI brand governance

### 1.3 Segment C: SEO Agencies & Consultants

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Size** | Solo consultants to 50-person specialized SEO firms |
| **Role** | SEO strategy, technical SEO, content optimization |
| **Primary Buyer** | Founder, Lead SEO Strategist, Agency Owner |
| **Pain Point** | Traditional SEO metrics (rankings, CTR) are incomplete; need AI visibility data |
| **Budget Authority** | $299-$999/month |
| **Decision Cycle** | 1-2 weeks; technical buyers, self-serve friendly |
| **Target Count (Year 1)** | 40-60 SEO specialists |
| **Primary Value** | AI citation data, prompt optimization recommendations, content gap analysis |

**Why They Buy:**
- SEO professionals are early adopters of new technology
- Clients demand AI-search optimization services
- Data-driven insights help SEOs justify strategy pivots
- Expanding service offering without large incremental investment

**Acquisition Channels:**
- SEO community platforms (Reddit r/SEO, Twitter/X SEO community)
- SEO tool integration partnerships (Ahrefs, SEMrush ecosystem)
- SEO conference sponsorships (BrightonSEO, SEJ Summit)
- SEO-focused content and tutorials on YouTube and blogs

## 2. Marketing Channels

### 2.1 ProductHunt

**Strategy:** BrandLens launch as a Product of the Day campaign.

| Detail | Plan |
|--------|------|
| **Launch Timing** | Tuesday, 12:01 AM PT (maximize 24-hour voting window) |
| **Pre-Launch Build-Up** | 2-week teaser campaign on LinkedIn/Twitter; collect 500+ upvote commitments |
| **Hunter Selection** | Target top 10 ProductHunt hunters (producthunt.com/@[top-hunter]) with exclusive early access |
| **Launch Day Team** | Founders + 3 team members + 20+ beta users actively upvoting and commenting |
| **Follow-Up** | Engage every comment personally; post "Top 5" badge on homepage |
| **Target** | Top 3 Product of the Day; 500+ upvotes |
| **Budget** | $0 (organic); optional $500 hunter referral fee |

- ProductHunt is the #1 discovery platform for B2B SaaS tools

- [ ] Close beta with 30-50 early users (SEO agencies, marketing freelancers)
- [ ] Gather testimonials and case study data
- [ ] Build waitlist with landing page (target 1,000 signups)
- [ ] Begin content production (10+ blog posts published)
- [ ] Establish social media presence (LinkedIn, Twitter/X)

- [ ] Finalize pricing and packaging based on beta feedback

- [ ] Recruit 5 ProductHunt hunters with warm introductions
- [ ] Create agency directory profiles
- [ ] Begin outreach to 100 target agencies via LinkedIn and email

## 6. Sales Process

### 6.1 Agency Tier — Self-Serve

1. **Awareness**: User discovers BrandLens via ProductHunt, search, or referral
2. **Trial**: 14-day free trial, no credit card required
3. **Onboarding**: Automated email sequence + interactive product tour
4. **Conversion**: In-app upgrade prompt at day 7 and day 12
5. **Expansion**: Monthly check-in emails; upgrade prompts at 80% query usage
6. **Retention**: Usage-based alerts; churn prevention at 30 days before downgrade

**Sales Involvement:** None required; sales-assisted only for deals > $500/mo

### 6.2 Enterprise Tier — Assisted

1. **Awareness**: User discovers via content, LinkedIn, conference, or referral
2. **Demo Request**: Form submission → inside sales schedules demo within 24 hours
3. **Demo**: 30-minute product demo tailored to use case
4. **Proposal**: Custom proposal sent within 48 hours of demo
5. **Trial/POC**: 14-day trial with dedicated onboarding support
6. **Close**: Contract sent via DocuSign; average 30-day close
7. **Onboarding**: White-glove onboarding session + dedicated CSM for first 90 days

**Sales Involvement:** Inside sales rep from demo through close

### 6.3 White-label Tier — Enterprise

1. **Awareness**: Inbound via agency directories or outbound prospecting
2. **Qualification**: SDR qualifies deal size and timeline
3. **Demo**: 45-minute solution architecture review
4. **Custom Proposal**: 3-7 day proposal development with custom terms
5. **POC**: 30-day proof-of-concept with white-label environment
6. **Close**: Legal review, custom contract, average 60-90 day close
7. **Onboarding**: Implementation project with dedicated solutions engineer

**Sales Involvement:** Enterprise AE from qualification through close; solutions engineering for technical evaluation

## 7. Customer Success & Retention

### 7.1 Onboarding

| Tier | Onboarding Experience |
|------|----------------------|
| Agency | Automated 5-step product tour + email sequence; 14-day trial |
| Enterprise | Dedicated onboarding call + custom query setup + team training |
| White-label | 2-4 week implementation project with solutions engineer |

### 7.2 Engagement Strategy

| Touchpoint | Agency | Enterprise | White-label |
|-----------|--------|------------|-------------|
| Welcome email | Day 0 | Day 0 | Day 0 |
| First value email (first citation found) | Day 3 | Day 3 | Day 1 |
| Usage report | Weekly | Monthly | Monthly |
| Check-in call | At 30 days | At 14, 30, 60, 90 days | Weekly for first month |
| Upgrade prompt | At 80% usage | At 6 months | Quarterly |

### 7.3 Churn Prevention

- **At-risk detection**: Flag customers with < 3 logins in 14 days or query usage < 20% of limit
- **Re-engagement campaign**: Personalized emails highlighting new features and missed insights
- **Exit survey**: Understanding churn reasons to improve product and retention
- **Win-back offers**: 30-day free reactivation for churned customers

### 7.4 Expansion Revenue Strategy

| Expansion Opportunity | Trigger | Offer |
|----------------------|---------|-------|
| Brand limit reached | 90% of brand slots used | Upgrade to next tier |
| Query volume exceeded | 85% of monthly queries used | Overage package or tier upgrade |
| New feature access | Feature launches | Invite to beta / limited access |
| Annual billing conversion | 11 months into monthly plan | 20% discount for annual commitment |
| White-label interest | Agency reselling signals | Custom white-label demo |
